Lifting is a first-class operation, not an administrative correction: a bounded action that cannot be undone before it expires is not bounded, it is just slow. A lift is appended to the ledger rather than deleting the hold, because "this account was frozen and then released" is the sentence an audit needs, and a table of current state cannot say it. Lifts carry no decision number — a release performed by a person is authorised by that person, and attributing it to a machine's decision would be a lie about who decided.
